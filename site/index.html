Features

Everything between your phone and PC.

Orbit sits quietly in your tray and keeps both devices in step.

Clipboard

Copy on one device, paste on the other.

Notifications

Your phone’s alerts, right on your PC.

Files

Drag, drop and send, either way.

Screen mirroring

Use your phone from your PC with mouse and keyboard.

Media controls

Play, pause and skip what’s playing on the other device.

Earbuds

Battery for any buds, noise control for realme and OnePlus.

Battery

See your phone’s charge at a glance.

USB modes

Charging, tethering or file sharing. Switch from either device.

Any connection

USB cable, your phone’s hotspot or the same Wi-Fi.

And more on the way.

Private by design

Just your two devices. Nothing in between.

Your phone and PC talk to each other directly, encrypted, over a cable or your own network.

No login

No account, no email, no password. Install, pair, done.

No servers

No cloud and no tracking. Your data never leaves your devices.

Open source

Free under AGPL-3.0. Every line of code is public.
