Connectivity

Connects Over Whatever Is Available

Orbit dials every route at once and keeps the first one that answers. No firewall rules, no port forwarding, no setup. Select a route to see it in action.

Connected over your phone’s hotspot With USB tethering or a hotspot, your phone is the PC’s gateway, so Orbit reaches it directly, typically in well under a second.

Security & Privacy

Private by Architecture

There is no Orbit server. Your data moves only between your own two devices, so there is nothing to sign up for, sync or breach.

No Account, No Cloud

Nothing to register and no relay in the middle. Orbit works on a cable or your own network, even with no internet.

Encrypted End to End

Every connection uses mutual TLS with certificates pinned at pairing. Only your paired devices can connect.

You Decide What Syncs

Choose which apps forward notifications and hide their text. Password-manager copies are never synced.

Open Source

No analytics, no ads. The complete source is public under AGPL-3.0, so you can audit and build it yourself.

Get Started

Up and Running in Minutes

Install Orbit on your PC. It sets up your phone for you over USB.

  1. 1

    Install Orbit for Windows

    Download the installer and run it. Orbit runs quietly in the system tray.

  2. 2

    Plug In Your Phone

    Turn on USB debugging, connect the cable and select Install on phone. Orbit installs the Android app and pairs both devices in one step.

  3. 3

    Grant Access

    Open Orbit on your phone and allow notification access. You’re connected.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Orbit really free?

Yes. Orbit is free and open source under the GNU AGPL-3.0, with no ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Orbit connects over a USB cable, USB tethering, your phone’s hotspot or a shared Wi-Fi network. Your data never leaves your two devices.

Why is the Android app installed from the PC?

In some regions, Play Protect blocks browser installs of apps that read notifications. Installing over USB from Orbit for Windows avoids this and pairs both devices in the same step. The APK is also attached to every release.

What does screen mirroring require?

USB debugging on your phone. Mirroring over Wi-Fi also works after a one-time wireless debugging setup in Orbit, with both devices on the same network. Mirroring is built on scrcpy.

Which earbuds are supported?

Any Bluetooth earbuds show battery and Play Here. Noise control, sound settings and tap actions are available for realme, OnePlus and OPPO models that use the realme Link or HeyMelody app.

Does Orbit support macOS, Linux or iPhone?

Not today. Orbit is built for Windows 10 and 11 and Android 10 or later, and connects one phone to one PC.
